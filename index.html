Training ground · one drill per technique

Farmor Ingrid explainsHow to play

  1. Throw the batons — six a turn, underhand, end over end. ←→ aim, hold Space: the mark runs out over the grass. Let go when the ring is green for a clean end-over-end spin; let go on red and it helicopters.
  2. Kubbs — the wooden blocks. Topple the kubbs on their half.
  3. Field kubbs — the kubbs they topple of yours, you throw onto their half, and stand up. You must topple those first. Put them side by side: one baton can take two. Skål!
  4. Field first — a baseline kubb knocked while a field kubb stands is stood up again.
  5. The advantage line — field kubbs left standing let the other side step up to the nearest one.
  6. The king — when every kubb on their half is down, topple the king from your baseline and you win. Hit him early and you lose.

Techniques — one unlocks per act

  1. Rakt! (from mission 2) — the vertical throw: let go in the gold core of the ring. The baton flies dead straight, skids on through and barely rolls on bumps.
  2. Drilla (from mission 6) — your inkast becomes hold-and-release: aim it right onto a lone field kubb of yours and let go on gold — it stands on top as a tower, and one baton fells both.
  3. The half-line king shot (from mission 11) — with their half clear, press ↑tap Step up to throw from 2 m. Only a gold release counts: anything else is a foul and your turn ends.

Act 2: the inkast — short of the middle line or out is thrown again; out twice is a straffkubb they stand where you like it least. They also raise your kubbs a kubb-length apart (towers and lines survive). Act 3: bumpy snow rolls the baton. Stars: ★ win · ★★ and ★★★ targets on each mission card; your rank comes from them.
